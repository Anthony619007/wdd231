Site Plan

Site Name

Ghana Vietnam Chamber of Commerce (GVCC)

Site Purpose

Based in Accra, Ghana, this website serves as the primary bilateral gateway to promote trade, investment, and economic cooperation between Ghana and Vietnam. It provides a formal registration hub for active businesses, cross-border legal resources, trade data, and local networking events to foster strong international commercial relations.

Target Market

Importers, exporters, manufacturing entities, cocoa and mineral logistics coordinators, and corporate executives operating out of Accra, Kumasi, Hanoi, and Ho Chi Minh City looking to expand cross-border business.

User Personas

1. Ghanaian Logistics Manager (Kwabena, 42)

Scenario: Needs to locate verified Vietnamese textile and agricultural suppliers. He visits the website to access the member directory and confirm trade compliance standards.

2. Vietnamese Agricultural Exporter (Nguyen, 35)

Scenario: Wants to register her business with the Chamber in Accra to secure local distribution networks. She uses the site to find membership fees and submit her online application.

3. Trade Attache / Investor (Adjoa, 50)

Scenario: Needs high-level updates on customs tariffs and bilateral agreements between the two nations. She checks the blog, news feeds, and calendar for upcoming trade summits.

SEO Plan

  • Keywords: Ghana Vietnam trade, Accra business directory, GVCC registration, bilateral commerce, Vietnam import export.
  • Verification: Register the physical Accra office on Google Business Profile.
  • Backlinks: Establish mandatory reciprocal profile linking on all registered member company websites.
  • Analytics: Integrate native Google Analytics scripts globally across all active pages.
